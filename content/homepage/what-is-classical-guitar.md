---
title: "What Is Classical Guitar?"
weight: 1
header_menu: true
---

The classical guitar is a nylon-string acoustic instrument, played sitting down
with the guitar raised on a footstool or support cushion and the strings
plucked directly by the fingertips and nails rather than with a pick.

That direct contact gives the player an unusual degree of control over tone and
dynamics, and because each finger moves independently, a single guitarist can
carry melody, bass, and accompaniment at once – the whole piece of music, not
just one part of it.

The repertoire stretches across five centuries, from Renaissance lute music and
Bach through the Spanish and Latin American traditions to composers writing
today.

It rewards patience, but even in the first few months you can play real music
that stands on its own.
